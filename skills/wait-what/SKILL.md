# Wait, what?

Someone did not follow. **Repeating it louder does not work** — and repeating it
at all, unchanged, wastes both people's time.

---

## ⛔ Diagnose before rephrasing

The instinct is to say it again with more words. Ask first which of these
happened:

| Symptom | Actual cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| they answer a different question | **wrong frame** — they are solving another problem | name their frame, then say where yours differs |
| "why would we do that?" | **missing why** — you gave the what | lead with the failure it prevents |
| they accept it but do the opposite | **no concrete anchor** | give one worked example |
| they push back on a detail | that detail is **load-bearing for them** | address it directly; do not zoom out |
| the same question twice | your answer **was not an answer** | answer the literal question first, in one line |
| silence, then a different plan | **too long** — they stopped reading | one paragraph, the conclusion first |

⚠️ **If you cannot tell which, ask.** One clarifying question costs less than a
second wrong explanation.

---

## Then re-pitch

**Change the shape, not the volume.**

- **Lead with the conclusion.** If they stop after one sentence, that sentence
  should be the one that matters.
- **Swap abstraction for a worked example** — a real case with real values beats
  another definition.
- **Say the failure, not the principle.** *"Two retries both succeed, the
  customer is charged twice, and they find out from their bank"* lands where
  *"data integrity matters"* does not.
- **Concede what is true in their reading** before diverging. If they were
  partly right, say so first — otherwise the correction reads as not listening.
- ⛔ **Do not re-send the original with more emphasis.** Bold does not create
  comprehension.

---

## 🔶 Consider that they are right

The most common reason a message does not land is that **it was wrong**.

Before re-pitching, spend one honest moment on: *if their reading is correct,
what would that mean?* If the answer is "they have a point", say that instead —
it is faster, and it is the thing that preserves the ability to disagree
usefully next time.

---

## Length

**One paragraph, or a short table.** If the re-pitch is longer than the original,
it will not be read either. Use `simple-writer` if installed for the rewrite itself.
